Privacy Policy for Listmate: Shared Lists

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Listmate: Shared Lists does not collect personal data. The developer cannot see your lists or any other information you store in the app.

Your lists live in your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service. Listmate has no custom server, advertising, analytics, or tracking. Your lists are shared only with people you choose to invite through Apple's iCloud sharing system. People you invite can see and edit the shared list.

Apple processes and stores iCloud data under Apple's terms and privacy policy. You control your lists through the app and your iCloud account.

Deleting your data: Delete a list in the app to remove it. To remove all Listmate data, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage (or Storage), and choose Listmate.

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted at the same web address with a new effective date.
